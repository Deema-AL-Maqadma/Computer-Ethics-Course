# Computer Ethics – Course Repository

This repository contains core course materials, research assignments, and exam resources for the **Computer Ethics** course.  
It provides a focused collection of documents covering key ethical principles, legal frameworks, and professional responsibilities in the computing field.

---

## 📚 Course Content & Overview

During the course, core topics in information technology ethics were covered through course lectures, research assignments, and final assessments, including:

- **Ethical Principles & Foundations**: Understanding ethics, morals, laws, and professional standards in IT.
- **Corporate & Social Responsibility**: Ethical decision-making frameworks, leadership, and code of conduct.
- **IT Professional Ethics**: Privacy, software compliance, security, and public policy issues.

---

## 📄 Repository Files

The repository includes the following key resources:

- **`Computer Ethics.pdf`**: Comprehensive course material and lecture notes covering foundational principles and professional conduct.
- **`Research Assignments.pdf`**: Collection of research tasks, case study analyses, and practical assignments.
- **`Computer_Ethics_Final.pdf`**: Final examination paper and assessment resources.
